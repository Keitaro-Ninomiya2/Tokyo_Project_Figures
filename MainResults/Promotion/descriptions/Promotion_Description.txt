Table:
This folder now mirrors the updated specification from Regressions/TransferOutcomes_Combined.R.

Panel A:
Transferred workers compared to destination-office peers already at the destination in the transfer year.
Treatment is Transferred (=1).
Controls include peer average rank, peer average tenure, peer female share, and log peer headcount.
Fixed effects are destination section, transfer year, and position.
Standard errors are clustered by destination section.

Panel B:
Transferred workers compared to donor-office peers who remained behind.
Treatment is Transferred (=1).
Controls include peer average rank, peer average tenure, peer female share, and log peer headcount.
Fixed effects are donor section, transfer year, and position.
Standard errors are clustered by donor section.

Panel C:
Transferees are compared within the same donor office x section x prior-position x year cell by whether the destination had a draft-induced vacancy.
Treatment is Draft-Induced Vacancy (=1).
Controls include worker productivity, tenure at transfer, and gender.
Productivity is a prior-year indicator built from court rank, decoration class, and standardized salary.
Fixed effects are donor cell x year and donor position.
Standard errors are clustered by donor cell.
Within each panel, all four outcomes are estimated on the same sample.
